About the Author ## Munashe Soka

Munashe Soka's writing ministry has experienced rapid growth since the release of his debut book, "The Hidden Man Of The Heart," in 2016. His subsequent titles have been met with wide appreciation and recognition across the global Christian community.

As a dedicated teacher of God's Word, Munashe's ministry is rooted in Bible-based teachings, faith, love, and a passion for soul-winning. His heart's desire is to spread the Gospel to the four corners of the earth and help believers understand the deep things of God.

Through his carefully crafted works, Munashe addresses critical issues facing the modern church, from financial stewardship and biblical giving to spiritual authority and the transformation of lives through the power of God's Word.
